# Rationale: Student Self Advocacy Intake Pass

## The Need for Patient Self Advocacy in Nigerian Clinics

Undergraduate health seekers in federal universities face significant power imbalances when dealing with institutional healthcare personnel. Students report being dismissed, hurried through consultations, or chastised for asking about prescribed drugs. This intimidation increases the risk of preventable medication errors, particularly involving known drug hypersensitivities such as penicillin anaphylaxis or Stevens Johnson syndrome triggers like cotrimoxazole.

By placing a clear, authoritative intake pass in the student's hands, MedWay transforms passive patients into active self advocates:
1. The pass establishes legitimate institutional routing through the MedWay platform.
2. Drug allergies are positioned front and center in bold warning colors that cannot be missed by the dispenser.
3. The plain text clipboard export allows students to share their intake details via SMS or WhatsApp with parents, hall wardens, or trusted peers in emergencies.

## Privacy and Zero Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

MedWay collects zero student names, matriculation numbers, phone numbers, or residential addresses on the intake pass. The pass only contains:
- An ephemeral random pass code: `MW-UNN-XXX`
- Target facility and care category
- Declared drug allergy warnings
- Timestamp of creation

This strict zero PII architectural design ensures that even if a student leaves their phone unlocked or shares a screenshot in a public group chat, their institutional privacy is never compromised.
