# Rationale: Auto Reroute Engine and Partner Directory

## Why Amber Reroute Banners

When a student arrives with fever and chills, finding out that medications are unavailable at the clinic causes acute distress. If the application silently places a private commercial pharmacy at the top of the list without explanation, the student may wonder why their free TISHIP clinic was replaced. 

An explicit amber banner answers this question directly:
1. It acknowledges that the university clinic does not have the medicine in stock.
2. It clarifies that MedWay has identified an accredited commercial partner that verified stock within the last four hours.
3. It informs the student of the expected retail cost range before they embark on the trip.

## Partner Accreditation and Gate Landmarks

Nigerian university campuses are often surrounded by informal medicine vendors, patent medicine vendors (PPMVs), and registered community pharmacies. MedWay only lists facilities accredited by the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) and verified by the MedWay field team. 

Furthermore, distance on campus is best understood by proximity to campus gates:
- Odim Gate: Closest to Franco, Eni Njoku, and Akpabio male hostels.
- Hilltop Gate: Closest to off campus student lodges and faculty clusters.
- Ogige Market Gate: Primary transit junction for commuter students.

Displaying gate landmarks alongside physical distance in meters dramatically reduces transit confusion for new students.
