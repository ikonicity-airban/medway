# 0003: Pure Institutional Monetization (Zero Counter Fees)

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-20

## Context & Problem Statement

Students are highly price-sensitive and will not pay subscriptions to check clinic queues. Furthermore, charging community pharmacies for placement introduces severe conflicts of interest that distort fair routing.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Pure Institutional Monetization", offering the platform 100% free to both students and accredited community pharmacies. Revenue is generated exclusively through high-value enterprise subscriptions for anonymized population health telemetry sold to TISHIP HMO syndicates, university health services, and epidemiological researchers.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Unbiased algorithmic routing; maximum viral student adoption and pharmacy participation; high-margin enterprise data contracts.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:** Requires initial bootstrapping before enterprise university health contracts are closed.

## Considered Options

- Pure Institutional Monetization (Chosen)
- Pharmacy SaaS Subscription with Priority Badges (Rejected: Violates neutral routing ethics and creates student distrust)
- Student Freemium / Paywalled Pass (Rejected: Causes immediate drop-off among sick undergraduates)
