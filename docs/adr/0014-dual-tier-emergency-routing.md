# 0014: Dual-Tier Emergency Destinations (UNN Medical Centre + Bishop Shanahan)

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-20

## Context & Problem Statement
When a student triggers a red-flag danger sign (difficulty breathing, persistent vomiting, severe acute pain), routing to retail pharmacies is immediately prohibited. The system must present reliable 24/7 emergency facilities capable of acute inpatient stabilization within Nsukka urban boundaries.

## Decision Outcome
Chosen option: "Dual-Tier Emergency Destinations", presenting UNN Medical Centre Emergency Casualty Bay (on-campus) and Bishop Shanahan Specialist Hospital (off-campus, Nsukka town), paired with 1-tap emergency telephone dialers, campus security dispatch lines, and turn-by-turn landmark directions.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Redundancy if on-campus casualty bay lacks power, oxygen, or specialized surgical capacity; immediate direct call capabilities.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:** Bishop Shanahan is a mission hospital requiring fee-for-service payment outside TISHIP coverage.

## Considered Options
- Dual-Tier Emergency Destinations (Chosen)
- UNN Campus Emergency Bay Only (Rejected: Single point of failure during campus strikes or facility power outages)
- Multi-Hospital Regional Directory (Rejected: Decision paralysis for a student experiencing an acute emergency)
