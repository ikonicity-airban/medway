# 0011: Institutional HMO and University Analytics Monetization

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-20

## Context & Problem Statement
Charging community pharmacies for placement or priority routing introduces severe ethical conflicts of interest and regulatory scrutiny. Furthermore, student-facing paywalls destroy viral adoption among cash-strapped undergraduates.

## Decision Outcome
Chosen option: "100% Free Pharmacy Network, Monetizing Strictly via Institutional TISHIP/HMO Analytics", maintaining zero fees for all students and partner pharmacies to achieve 100% campus inventory coverage. The platform monetizes by licensing aggregated, anonymized population health trends, peak disease incidence, and drug stockout telemetry to university health administration, HMO syndicates, and public health researchers.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Zero conflict of interest in algorithmic routing; friction-free onboarding for 100% of local pharmacies; maximum student trust; high-value enterprise B2B/B2G contracts.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:** Longer sales cycle to secure institutional university and HMO contracts compared to transactional retail subscriptions.

## Considered Options
- 100% Free Pharmacy Network, Monetizing via Institutional Analytics (Chosen)
- Flat-Rate SaaS for Verified Badge (Rejected: Causes onboarding hesitation among small independent pharmacies)
- Pay-Per-Routed-Student Model (Rejected: Creates perverse commercial incentives to over-prescribe or route to highest bidder)
