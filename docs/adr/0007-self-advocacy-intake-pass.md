# 0007: Zero-IT Student Self-Advocacy Intake Pass

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-20

## Context & Problem Statement
UNN Medical Centre operates with paper patient folders, manual queues, and heavily overburdened triage nurses. Requiring triage nurses to adopt a digital QR scanner or log into a web app creates high operational resistance and risks immediate counter-level abandonment.

## Decision Outcome
Chosen option: "Student Self-Advocacy Slip (Zero Staff Dependency)", formatting the Digital Pre-Visit Intake Pass (`MW-UNN-XXX`) as a high-contrast, offline-accessible student summary containing declared allergies, symptom duration, and requested non-diagnostic categories so the student can verbally read or present the physical phone screen to the nurse, requiring zero technical integration from clinic personnel.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Zero institutional dependency on day one; provides immediate utility to students communicating symptoms under acute stress; eliminates counter-staff technology barriers.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:** Does not automatically update hospital intake registries or speed up paper folder retrieval in the hospital archives.

## Considered Options
- Student Self-Advocacy Slip (Zero Staff Dependency) (Chosen)
- Physical Thermal / Paper Printout Station at Clinic Entrance (Rejected: Requires dedicated hardware maintenance, paper roll refills, and physical security)
- Single Shared Nurse Tablet / Webhook Dashboard (Rejected: High risk of loss, battery drain, and clinic staff resistance)
