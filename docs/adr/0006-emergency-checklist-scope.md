# 0006: Expanded Emergency Checklist with Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-11-20

## Context & Problem Statement

A narrow 4-item emergency checklist risks generating false clinical reassurance, leading students with acute life-threatening symptoms (such as acute severe abdominal pain, acute hemorrhage, or trauma) to believe they have been clinically cleared to purchase over-the-counter medications at an off-campus retail chemist.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Explicit Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer + Emergency Ward Override", expanding the WHO checklist to incorporate "Severe Acute Pain or Uncontrolled Bleeding" and attaching an unambiguous, persistent disclaimer: "This is not a clinical clearance; seek emergency hospital care if your condition worsens or pain is severe."

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Closes a major clinical safety loophole; protects students from delayed emergency escalation while maintaining a strict non-diagnostic boundary.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:** Adds an additional checklist item; increases the rate of emergency ward redirection for ambiguous acute pain symptoms.

## Considered Options

- Explicit Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer + Emergency Ward Override (Chosen)
- Strict Triage Lockout for Any Unlisted Severe Symptoms (Rejected: Causes excessive false-positive lockouts for minor conditions)
- Minimalist 4-Item WHO Screen with Standalone Pharmacy Advisory (Rejected: Unacceptable clinical liability in acute abdominal or bleeding emergencies)
